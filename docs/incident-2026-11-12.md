Osiris Firewatch — Real-World Recovery Test

Date: November 12, 2026
Version: v0.2.0-alpha
Environment: Ubuntu Linux, Docker, systemd

Overview

Osiris Firewatch underwent an unplanned real-world test following a Windscribe VPN disconnection and subsequent server reboot.

Despite losing external connectivity, Firewatch continued monitoring infrastructure independently of the GNOME desktop session.

Results

Firewatch successfully detected the VPN outage, queued undeliverable Discord alerts, and continued monitoring local services.

Following the reboot, Firewatch automatically initiated recovery for two unhealthy Docker containers.

Identified Limitations

* VPN dependency: Windscribe appears to depend on the graphical desktop session.
* Notifications: Discord alerts could not be delivered during the outage. Delivery of queued alerts remains unverified.
* VPN recovery: Firewatch detected the failure but did not demonstrate automatic VPN reconnection.

Conclusion

This incident validated Firewatch’s ability to detect failures, operate without an active desktop session, and automatically recover Docker services.

The monitoring cycle completed with zero failures.

Future development will focus on unattended VPN recovery and more resilient alert delivery.

Post-Incident Update — November 12, 2026

Following the Ubuntu update and server reboot, Osiris Firewatch successfully recovered two Docker containers. Delayed Discord notifications were subsequently delivered.

16-hour follow-up: All monitored services remained stable, with zero additional failures reported.

This provides further evidence of Firewatch’s recovery capabilities and post-incident stability.
